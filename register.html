Create an account

Two fields and a password. It takes about twenty seconds, and it is never required to buy anything.

What it is for

    What we store

    Your email, and a name if you give one. That is the whole record.

    What we never store

    • Card details — payment happens on our provider's own page
    • Your address, until you give one at checkout
    • Anything at all if you would rather check out as a guest

    Changed your mind?

    Write to us and the account is deleted the same day, along with everything in it.
