Denim is chosen on its weight long before its colour. Ounces per square yard decide whether a piece falls or holds, how long it takes to break in, and what it will look like in a year.

The maison

Twenty-six hours,
three pairs of hands

A VOLÀ corset passes through the pattern room, the boning bench and the finisher before it is ever seen. Twelve spiral-steel bones. Silk charmeuse against the skin, denim against the world. We make each to order, and we number each one.

We buy whole bolts from four mills, so every piece can be traced to the run it was woven in — and so, when a cloth is gone, the piece is retired with it.

Why VOLÀ

Cut on the stand

Every pattern is draped in three dimensions before it is ever flattened. It is why the leg falls the way it does.

Four mills, no more

Albini, Berto, Kaihara, Candiani, Kuroki. Long relationships, whole bolts, and full traceability on every metre.

Numbered, not mass-made

Atelier pieces are produced in editions of sixty or fewer, each stamped with its number inside the waistband.

Repaired for life

Send any VOLÀ piece back to Florence and we will mend it. Complimentary, for as long as you own it.

Private appointments

Be measured in Florence

Made-to-measure corsetry and denim tailoring, by appointment at Via dei Fossi. Two fittings, six weeks, one piece that is only yours.
