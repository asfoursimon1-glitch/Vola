Cloth that moves

Raw denim does not stay the size you bought

Most of the collection is washed and settled before it reaches you. A few pieces are not, and those will shrink at the first wash and stretch back with wear. Buy them with that in mind rather than to fit perfectly on day one.

If you sit between two sizes on more than one piece, a commission will cost less in the long run than two returns and a compromise. The consultation is free.
