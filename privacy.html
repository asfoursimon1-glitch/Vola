      Your measurements

      The Fit Studio asks for your height, weight, waist and shoe size so it can recommend a size. This is the most personal thing this site handles, so it is handled differently from everything else:

      • It stays in your browser. The profile is saved on your device and used there. Nothing about your body is sent to us by saving it.
      • It is never attached to a message unless you tick a box. The contact and commission forms offer to include your saved sizes. The box is off by default, and it prints the exact line that would be sent before you tick it.
      • An order carries only what it needs. If you have a profile saved, the sizes line travels with a checkout as a note to the atelier — the size, not the measurements it was derived from.
      • Erase it in one click, in the section above or from the Fit Studio itself.

      Under the GDPR, measurements of your body are ordinary personal data rather than a special category — but they are the sort of thing people are entitled to feel particular about, and the design treats them that way.

      These are yours under the GDPR whether or not a policy lists them. Exercising any of them is free, and we will answer within one month.

      How to ask

      We will not ask you to explain why. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to your national data protection authority — in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

      Transfers outside the EEA

      Several of the services listed above are American companies. Where your data reaches one of them, it leaves the European Economic Area. Each is relied upon under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses; which one applies to which service has to be confirmed against each provider's current terms rather than asserted here.

      The one avoidable transfer on this site is named in the table above: the fonts.
