Made to Measure

A piece cut to your body alone, on a pattern that exists only for you. Two fittings across six weeks at Via dei Fossi 14.

Why commission

A pattern cut
for one body

Ready-to-wear is a compromise made in advance — a shape drafted for an average and graded outwards. It fits most people adequately and nobody exactly.

A commission removes the compromise. We measure you, cut a pattern that exists only for you, and fit the piece on your body twice before it is finished. Rise, leg line, sweep, boning position, hem — all of it is yours to set. The pattern is kept, so a second commission begins where the first left off.
